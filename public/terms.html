Forries Rewards – Terms & Conditions

Effective 20 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

In short
  • Membership is free and for people 18 and older.
  • Show your live card when you order; with a valid till slip you get one stamp per card per visit.
  • A full card earns the reward shown on it. Rewards have no cash value, can’t be transferred and expire on the date shown in the app.
  • One reward per visit, and rewards can’t be redeemed on major rugby match days announced in the app.
  • We only send marketing if you opt in, and you can delete your account and data yourself at any time. See the Privacy Notice.

1. About the programme

  1. Forries Rewards (“the programme”) is run by Primeinvest 1069 (Pty) Ltd, trading as Foresters Arms (“Forries”, “we”, “us”), 52 Newlands Avenue, Newlands, Cape Town, 7700.
  2. The programme is free to join. By joining you agree to these terms and our Privacy Notice.
  3. The programme is valid only at Foresters Arms, 52 Newlands Avenue, Newlands.

2. Membership

  1. You must be 18 years or older to join. We may ask for proof of age before giving you any reward.
  2. One membership per person. Your membership and card are personal to you and may not be shared, sold or transferred.
  3. Please keep your details up to date in the app. We may contact you by email about your membership (for example, sign-in codes and changes to these terms).

3. Earning stamps

  1. To earn a stamp, show the live QR code in the app (or your Google Wallet card) to a staff member when you order. The code changes every 30 seconds; screenshots and old codes are not accepted.
  2. Stamps are given for a qualifying purchase on a valid Foresters Arms till slip. Each till slip can be used for one member only, and once per stamp card.
  3. You can earn one stamp per stamp card per visit, and no more than the daily limit shown in the app.
  4. Stamps must be added at the time of purchase. We can’t add stamps for past purchases.
  5. Stamps and rewards have no cash value and can’t be exchanged for cash, credit or other goods, or combined with other members’ cards.
  6. Staff decisions on whether a purchase qualifies are final. If a stamp is added by mistake, we may remove it.

4. Rewards

  1. When you fill a stamp card you receive the reward shown on that card. Rewards appear in the app and are valid until the expiry date shown there (normally 90 days).
  2. One reward may be redeemed per visit.
  3. Rewards can’t be redeemed on major rugby match days. We announce these “no-reward days” in the app in advance. You still earn stamps on those days, and any reward that would expire on a no-reward day stays valid until the next day you can use it.
  4. To redeem, show your live card and tell the staff member which reward you’d like. A reward is used up once a staff member redeems it in the app.
  5. Rewards that include alcohol are only for members aged 18 or older and are subject to responsible service of alcohol. Staff may refuse to serve alcohol to anyone who appears intoxicated, and may offer a non-alcoholic alternative.
  6. Rewards are subject to availability. If a reward item is unavailable, we will offer a reasonable alternative of similar value.
  7. Rewards can’t be exchanged for cash, transferred, or used together with other promotions unless we say otherwise.
  8. Birthday treat: if you give us your birthday, you receive a birthday reward valid for your birthday month. You can only set your birthday once in the app; contact us if it needs correcting.

5. Misuse

  1. We may suspend or close a membership, and cancel its stamps and rewards, if we reasonably believe the programme has been misused. Examples include sharing cards, using someone else’s till slips, fake or altered slips, or abusing staff.
  2. Where possible we’ll tell you why, and you can ask us to review the decision by emailing admin@forries.co.za.

6. Changes to the programme and these terms

  1. We may change the stamp cards, rewards or these terms. We’ll give reasonable notice in the app or by email before a change that affects you negatively takes effect.
  2. We may end the programme by giving at least 30 days’ notice. Rewards you have already earned will remain valid until their expiry date or the end of the notice period, whichever is later.
  3. You can leave at any time by deleting your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account and data). Unused stamps and rewards are lost when you do.

7. General

  1. The app is provided “as is”. We do our best to keep it running, but we’re not responsible for losses caused by temporary outages, your phone or network. If a technical fault affects your stamps or rewards, let us know and we will put it right.
  2. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 68 of 2008, or any other law.
  3. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
  4. Questions or complaints: admin@forries.co.za, or speak to the manager on duty.

Privacy Notice (POPIA)

This notice explains how Primeinvest 1069 (Pty) Ltd t/a Foresters Arms (the “responsible party”) handles your personal information in Forries Rewards, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

InformationWhy
Name, email addressTo create and run your membership, and send sign-in codes and important account messages.
Mobile number (optional)To find your membership when you sign in, and for WhatsApp messages if you opt in.

Marketing

We only send specials and event news by email, WhatsApp or notifications if you opt in, and each channel is a separate choice. You can change your choices any time in Profile, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We use trusted service providers who process it on our behalf under contract:

  • Google (Firebase) hosts the app and database. Your data is stored in Google’s Johannesburg, South Africa data centre; some processing (such as the daily birthday job and web traffic routing) passes through Google servers in Europe.
  • xneelo sends our emails.
  • If you choose to use them: Apple / Google Wallet and your phone’s notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), and Meta (WhatsApp) if you opt in to WhatsApp messages.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a member. If you delete your account, your profile, stamps, rewards and activity history are deleted immediately. Records we must keep by law (if any) are kept only as long as required. Till slip records used for fraud checks are kept for up to 12 months.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to authorised staff with individual logins, and every staff action is logged.

Your rights

  • See and correct your details in the app (Profile), or ask us for a copy of the information we hold.
  • Delete your account and data yourself at any time (Profile → Delete my account and data).
  • Object to marketing at any time, which we will always respect.
  • Complain to us at admin@forries.co.za, or to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
